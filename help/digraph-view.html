The Digraph View

The Opening Graph is a network diagram of your repertoire: each node is a position (a "room"), each edge is a move between positions, labeled with how often that reply has actually occurred in your imported games.

Reading the graph

Node labels carry small status glyphs so you can scan a whole castle at a glance: 🧠 means the room is marked memorized, 🎨 means it's fully decorated (every prepared reply has a real image, every named door leads somewhere named). A room with both shows a distinct border as well.

Focusing a castle

On a large repertoire, use the "Show Castle" dropdown to narrow the view to one castle's rooms instead of the whole tree.

Jumping into VR

Click any node to open its room-info panel -- name, exits, and a "Jump to VR" button that teleports you straight into that room in the 3D walkthrough, without navigating the street by hand.
