Settings

Menu → Settings holds the few preferences that belong to this browser rather than to your repertoire. Changes apply straight away.

Reviews

Notify me when a same-day review is due turns on desktop notifications, with a Send a test notification button and a line saying when the last reminder went out and how. See Marking Rooms as Memorized for how reminders work.

Slowdowns

A list of the times the page stopped responding for more than a second since it was opened, and what was running then -- for example building castles, or a background scan. When a click seems to take ages, this says what the page was busy with, which is the first thing needed to fix it.
