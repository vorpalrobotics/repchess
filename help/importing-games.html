Importing Your Games

Your own games are what populate the move tree with real, played moves and their frequencies -- REPchess uses them to show which replies are actually worth preparing for.

From Lichess or Chess.com

Menu → Import → Games. Enter a username and choose a source: Lichess (by game count) or Chess.com (by number of recent monthly archives). The first import establishes your identity for this browser -- later imports, even from the other platform, add to the same repertoire rather than starting a new one.

Both sources capture full game details -- date, both players, ratings, result, and a link back to the original game -- not just the moves, so you can dig back into any of them later. See Exploring Your Games for what that unlocks.

Pasting variations

Menu → Import → Variations lets you paste move lists directly (useful for lines from a book, a database, or a friend) without needing a full game file.

Local game files

A PGN or similar export from another tool can also be imported directly, merging into your existing games rather than replacing them.
