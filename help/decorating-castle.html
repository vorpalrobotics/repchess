Decorating Your Castle

A generated castle's rooms are structurally complete but empty -- the memory-palace technique needs something memorable in each one, which is what decorating adds.

Edit mode

Inside a room, click the pencil icon to enter edit mode (Esc leaves it again). Each room has one "move-object slot" per prepared reply out of that position -- these are the objects you decorate to build your mental associations.

Assigning content to a slot

Click an empty slot to open the asset picker: assign one of your mnemonic images, a different VR asset, or (if you don't have an image yet) a plain typed placeholder label as a stand-in. An object-list "bucket" can also drive a whole wall's worth of slots at once, in order.

Once a slot holds something -- an image or just a typed label -- clicking it instead selects it for moving: arrow keys nudge its position, +/- resize it, and the gear icon that appears (or the Enter key) reopens the picker to change the image/label or remove it.

Fitting the room

Room Geometry lets you resize a room to fit what you've placed in it; the crop editor (brush and bucket erase, undo/redo) helps clean up an image before it goes on the wall.

Knowing when you're done

A room is "fully decorated" -- shown with a 🎨 badge, both in the VR toolbar and on the Opening Graph -- once every move-object slot has a real asset and every named door's destination room has a name.
