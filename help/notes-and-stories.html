Notes and Stories

A position can carry two pieces of writing. They are kept apart because they do different jobs:

  • The note is about the chess: the threat to watch for, the plan for both sides, how the game tends to go once your preparation runs out.
  • The story is about remembering: the little scene that links a move's images together so the sequence sticks.

A room can also have a room story of its own: the scene that ties the whole room together.

All three are written in Markdown, in a formatting editor, so headings, lists and bold text work. Plain text still reads exactly as you typed it, line breaks included.

A note belongs to a position

A note is attached to the position, not to the order of moves that reached it. Two lines that transpose into the same position share one note, whether you open it from the move table or from the VR walk.

In the move table

  • The three-dot menu on a row has Notes…, which opens the note editor directly.
  • A row with a note shows a note icon. Click it to read or edit the note.
  • Set Attributes shows a preview of the note and of the story, each with its own edit button.

In the VR walk

  • On a move pair: walk up close to a move pair's images and look at it, and a small chessboard icon appears on its corner. The icon looks different when there is a note to read. Click it for that position's board, with its note and story beside it. The pencil beside each one edits it in place, without leaving the walk.
  • At a dead end: a scroll hangs beside a "no continuation" sign when the line's last move has a note. It opens the same note, so you can read what happens next while standing at the end of the line. The scroll stays visible with hints turned off, because it gives away no moves.
  • Room story: the book icon in the lower-right corner of the walk opens the current room's story, and lets you write or edit it. The icon is there in every castle room, so you can start a story anywhere. It is bright yellow when the room has a story and dim when it doesn't have one yet.

The move-pair icons and dead-end scrolls don't appear in edit mode, where clicks go to picking and placing objects.

Kept safe

Notes, move stories and room stories are all included in a full backup.
