Building Your Repertoire

A repertoire is organized into opening systems ("lines") -- each one covers a family of positions that share a trigger, such as "1. d4" or "any first move." Create one from the home screen with the + button: give it a name, a color, and the move(s) that trigger it.

The move tree

Opening an system shows a tree of positions built from your imported games. Each row is a position reached by a sequence of moves; expanding a row shows the replies that have actually occurred, with how often each one was played.

Standard responses

The core idea in REPchess: for any position where it's your move, you pick one reply as your prepared, memorized answer -- the "standard response." That choice is what later becomes a room and a door in the VR memory palace, and what a board-play test will expect you to find.

You don't have to prepare everything at once -- build out a system as deep as you like, and leave the rest for later.
