Exploring Your Games

Beyond feeding the move tree's frequencies, your imported games are their own source of insight -- both available from any row's ⋮ menu in the move table.

Games with this Position

Lists your own games that reached that row's position, with date, opponent, rating, result, and the move you played -- a pawn or knight badge marks whether each came from Chess.com or Lichess, and clicking a row opens it on the original site. Choose Any transposition to match the position by any move order, or This line only for an exact match of your prepared sequence. A summary line reports your overall score from these games, plus how often you actually followed your own standard response when one's set.

Compare to Actual Games

Toggles commentary lines right under the row, showing which moves you've actually played from that exact position in your own games -- grouped with a play count, sorted by how often you played each. Whatever matches your current standard response is left out, so this only surfaces divergence: places where practice and preparation disagree.

If a row has no standard response configured yet, nothing is excluded, and a Use as Standard button appears on your most-played alternate -- a quick way to set a new node's reply from what you've actually been doing rather than typing one in cold.

Each alternate also has a small board icon to run the engine on it right there, showing a live score alongside the move -- handy for checking whether a move you've drifted into is still objectively sound before deciding whether to adopt it as the new standard.
