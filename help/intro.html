Welcome to REPchess

REPchess is a chess opening-repertoire trainer built around a uniquely memorable idea: a walkable 3D "memory palace" that turns your opening lines into a place you can actually explore.

Build a repertoire

Import games from Lichess or Chess.com, paste in variations, or build lines move by move against the tree view. Every position can carry engine evaluation, notes, and a chosen "standard response" for your own repertoire moves.

Walk your openings in VR

Each castle in the memory palace maps a branch of your repertoire onto a building you can walk through -- one room per position, one door per reply. Rooms can be decorated with objects and images tied to your own memory techniques, so recalling a line becomes recalling a place.

Mnemonics

Assign a word, description, and image to any piece landing on any square, then reuse them to decorate rooms in the memory palace or drill them directly in Manage Mnemonics.

Engine analysis

Analyze any position live, or queue deep background analysis across many positions at once -- the Analysis Queue keeps working while you do something else, and results are saved straight onto the repertoire tree.

Test yourself

Quiz sessions play the opponent's side and expect you to find your own prepared responses, scoped to a whole opening system, a single castle, or just the rooms you've marked as memorized.
