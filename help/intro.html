Welcome to REPchess

REPchess is a chess opening-repertoire builder and trainer designed around a uniquely memorable idea: a walkable 3D "memory palace" that turns your opening lines into a place you can actually explore.

STEP 1: Build a repertoire

Import games from Lichess or Chess.com to see what your opponents play against you, paste in variations from books or other sources, or build lines move by move against the tree view. Use a strong multithreaded engine to evaluate multiple lines and choose your preferred response. Every position can carry engine evaluation, notes, and a chosen "standard response" for your own repertoire moves.

Walk your openings in VR

Memory palaces, or castles, are generated automatically from your tree of moves and responses. You can mark related lines as belonging to a named castle. So each castle in the memory palace maps a branch of your repertoire onto a building you can walk through -- one room per position, one door per reply. Rooms can be decorated with objects and images tied to your own memory techniques, so recalling a line becomes recalling a place and mentally "walking" through it.

Mnemonics

Assign a word, description, and image to any piece landing on any square, then reuse them to decorate rooms in the memory palace or drill them directly in Manage Mnemonics. REPchess provides a complete set of default mnemonic images based on the Major System, but you can design your own if you desire. You can also provide room decorations to make each one distinct in your mind and help anchor the move images to objects. Hundreds of assets are provided by default: wall, floor, and ceiling textures, furniture, wall hangings, sculpture, household appliances, and more. You can upload your own assets or even generate them directly in REPchess using your own GPT API key.

Engine analysis

In the move table view of your repertoire you can analyze any position live, or queue deep background analysis across many positions at once -- the Analysis Queue keeps working while you do something else, and results are saved straight onto the repertoire tree, allowing you to select your standard responses based on strong engine analysis.

Test yourself

Quiz sessions play the opponent's side and expect you to recall your own prepared responses, scoped to a whole opening system, a single castle, or just the rooms you've marked as memorized. There is also a quiz mode to help you learn the standard move images so you can instantly recall the move associated with each image.

A Comprehensive System

In short, REPchess is a revolutionary system for managing your openings, choosing responses to the moves your opponents actually have played against you (or theoretical lines you've never actually seen over the board), and then allows you to use the most powerful known memory system available: the memory palace technique, which is thousands of years old and proven to allow anyone to memorize massive amounts of data and retain it for years. Rather than use an existing structure, the system creates a room layout that exactly matches your opening. The ability to have multiple memory castles means you can memorize complete opening systems involving hundreds or even thousands of variations.
