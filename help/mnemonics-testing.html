Move Mnemonics: Testing Yourself

Menu → Test → Mnemonics runs a quiz over your stored words/images -- a lightweight way to drill your memory-palace vocabulary without walking the VR world.

Scope

You can quiz over every mnemonic you've stored, or scope the session to just the squares/pieces actually needed by a chosen opening system or castle, so you're only drilling what's relevant to what you're building.

Scoring

Each session tracks hits and misses as you go, giving a quick read on which words and images have actually stuck.
