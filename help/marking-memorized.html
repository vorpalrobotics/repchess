Marking Rooms as Memorized

Decorating a room is only half the job -- at some point you actually know it. Marking a room "memorized" records that milestone.

Toggling the flag

The brain icon in the VR toolbar marks the current room memorized. It's stored per room and persists across reloads, and shows up as a 🧠 glyph on that room's node in the Opening Graph, so you can see at a glance how much of a castle you've actually learned versus just built.

Reviewing what you've memorized

Memorizing a room isn't the end of it -- memory fades, and the cure is to come back at widening intervals. Once a room is memorized the brain icon becomes its review light: green while the schedule is satisfied, amber when a review is due, red once it's well overdue. Walk in, quiz yourself on the room from memory, then say how it went -- press 1 for recalled perfectly, 2 for mostly correct, 3 for a failure. (Clicking the brain offers the same three choices, plus unmarking the room.)

Each grade moves the room along a fixed ladder of intervals -- 1, 3, 7, 21, 60, then 180 days. A perfect recall advances a step, a failure drops the room back to the start, and "mostly correct" holds it where it is -- twice in a row, though, and it steps back, since an interval you keep half-missing is too long. Due dates get a small random spread so a wing memorized in one sitting doesn't come due all on the same day forever after.

Grading the same room twice in one visit replaces the first grade rather than stacking on it, so a mis-keyed 1 when you meant 2 is simply fixed by pressing 2. Unmarking a room memorized clears its review history too.

Using it while testing

Both the mnemonics quiz and board-play testing can be scoped to "only memorized" rooms, so a review session drills what you've already committed to memory instead of mixing in material you're still building out.

It also protects the room's layout

Marking a corridor or two-track room memorized locks its shape in: if a new variation later adds a reply partway through it, that new reply becomes a side-door instead of splitting the room and reshuffling what you've already memorized. A memorized room with a branch inside it instead (not a simple corridor/two-track) shows a ⚠️ warning glyph on the Opening Graph when a new variation lands in it, since its layout can't be frozen the same way -- worth a look before you trust your memory of it.
