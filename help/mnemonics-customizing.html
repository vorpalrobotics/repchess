Move Mnemonics: Customizing Your Images

Any square/piece mnemonic can be replaced with your own word, description, and picture -- the default set is a starting point, not a fixed one.

Editing a square

Open Manage Mnemonics, click a cell, and set the word/description/image for that piece and square. Upload your own image, or use the built-in crop editor to trim, erase (brush or bucket/flood-fill), and undo/redo before saving it.

Narrowing to what you actually need

The mnemonic vocabulary is global -- 64 squares × 6 pieces -- but any one castle asks for a small slice of it. Pick an opening system or a single castle under Repertoire coverage, and the grid narrows to that scope.

In the word view (ABC) that scope drives the red/green colouring: green for a square/piece the scope needs and you've filled in, red for one it needs that's still missing. In a piece view -- one of the piece icons, showing that piece's picture on every square -- the squares that piece never lands on inside the scope are greyed right back instead, leaving just the images that castle actually calls for, with a count beside the dropdown. They stay clickable: filling one in ahead of time is fine, it's simply not work this castle is waiting on.

With (none selected) nothing is narrowed and the whole vocabulary is treated as needed, which makes the same views double as a global "what's still missing" check.

Bulk import

Menu → Import → Move Images accepts a batch of PNG files named like Nf6.png, assigning each straight to its matching piece/square in one pass -- handy after generating a whole set of images elsewhere.

Backing up just your mnemonics

Menu → Export → Mnemonics saves a standalone mnemonics bundle (words, descriptions, images, and notes); its Import button, inside Manage Mnemonics itself, restores one -- separately from a full repertoire backup, useful for sharing a set or keeping your own images safe independent of games and opening systems.
